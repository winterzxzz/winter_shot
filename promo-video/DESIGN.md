# MacScreenShot, “Your screen. Beautifully told.”

## Brief
- Audience: Mac users, developers and designers who share screenshots and product demos.
- Outcome: make the free, private capture-to-polish workflow immediately understandable.
- Format: 1920×1080, 60 fps, H.264 + AAC, approximately 60–70 seconds.
- Source of truth: refreshed repository README and the seven current screenshots. No old demo media.
- Tone: confident, warm, precise. Spacious product-film pacing, not a feature checklist.
- Palette: near-black #090811, white #F6F3FF, muted lavender #B5AEC8, purple #9670FF.
- Type: bundled DM Sans. Large, short headlines; restrained labels. No fabricated UI.

## Story and scenes
1. Hook: “Your screen. Beautifully told.” App icon and captured UI.
2. Capture: Command Shift Four, frozen selector. Slow push toward the selected window.
3. Annotate: nine tools, non-destructive edits. Zoom toward toolbar and annotations.
4. Beautify: real gradient screenshot. Steady reveal; rounded corners and shadow.
5. Record: display, window, area. Actual timer HUD with a red recording accent.
6. Studio: optional editor, auto-zoom and synthetic cursor. Real editor overview then detail.
7. Export: backdrops and 60 fps. Focus on studio controls, not simulated live interaction.
8. History: screenshot/recording library and notch history. Wide panel with subtle drift.
9. Privacy: one Screen Recording permission; no account or telemetry; captures stay on-device.
10. CTA: free, MIT licensed, GitHub download address held long enough to read.

## Motion
- Ease-out 0.65-second entrance, 0.32-second scene handoff, approximately 0.09-second text staggers.
- Keep the first frame meaningful. Avoid fade-to-black between scenes.
- Screenshots use restrained 2–7% Ken Burns moves or deliberate detail reframing.
- Full interface shown before details; screenshot motion is editorial, not a live UI demonstration.
- Persistent branding and a small chapter marker tie the film together.

## Sound
- Compare Kokoro full-precision ONNX voices af_heart, af_bella and am_michael on the same line.
- Prefer an unhurried, warm stock voice, not an imitation of a named person.
- Audition at speed 0.94; generate final scenes at 0.90 for more deliberate pacing. Verify text with local Whisper.
- Trim outer silence conservatively; apply mild high-pass/EQ, de-essing and compression.
- Target final -16 LUFS integrated and true peak at or below -1 dBTP.
- No music: clean voice-first presentation avoids unnecessary licensing and masking.
- Auditory naturalness cannot be fully judged through this tool interface. Numerical/ASR checks
  are evidence, not a claim of human listening. Preserve candidates for human audition.

## Review and finish
Inspect timeline and SVG diagnostics, render scene contact sheets and full-size frames, inspect
entrance/transition strips, then render the requested final export. Verify encoded video and
decoded AAC loudness, duration, fps, frame count, and local ASR coverage. No test suite.

## Source qualifications
The refreshed selector is a current-source rendered harness capture, not a live desktop capture.
The studio screenshot uses the staged synthetic Northstar recording described in the prior
README handoff. The promo uses these supplied assets without claiming they are live footage.
“Nothing leaves your Mac” refers to captures and processing; the app checks GitHub for updates.
